HELP - FIND YOUR PROBLEM FAST
=============================

I want to put the software on my watch ..................... help/2_FLASHING.txt
The computer does not see the watch ......................... Use a USB-C DATA cable. Try another USB port. Hold BOOT while
                                                              plugging in. (help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, A)
The screen is black .......................................... help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, B
Touch does nothing ........................................... help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, C
No sound ..................................................... help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, D
Wi-Fi finds nothing ......................................... help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, E
Bluetooth finds are slow .................................... SETUP > Bluetooth share (help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, E)
The watch face says "clock not set" ......................... Open the Live page from your phone (help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, F)
My phone cannot join the watch's Wi-Fi ...................... help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, G
How do I update to a newer release? ......................... Flash the new .bin again, or use the phone update page (help/2_FLASHING.txt)
Anything else ................................................ help/4_FAQ.txt and the user guide (docs folder)

If it is still stuck, open an issue on the project page (Bug report form). Include the release name shown on the
boot screen, what you did, and what the Serial Monitor (115200 baud) printed.
