3. TROUBLESHOOTING
==================

The watch prints start-up messages over USB that help a lot: open any serial terminal at 115200 baud on its port.
The watch's own check: SETUP > Self-test (green dot = healthy, red dot = not, grey = a part your watch does not have).

A. The computer does not see the watch / flashing fails
   - Use a USB-C DATA cable. Try another USB port on the computer.
   - Hold the BOOT side button while plugging the cable in, release it, then try again.
   - If the connection is unreliable, add --baud 115200 to the command.

B. Nothing shows on the screen
   - Unplug the cable, wait 10 seconds, plug it in again. Try another cable or USB port.
   - Flash the .bin again (help/2_FLASHING.txt).
   - If the watch seems off: hold PWR for 6 seconds until it is off, then press PWR once to start it.

C. Touch does nothing
   - The boot screen says "touch NOT detected": unplug, wait, plug in again. Check SETUP > Self-test > Touch.
   - The first touch after a pause does nothing: normal, that touch wakes the screen.

D. No sound
   Check in this order:
   1. the Volume slider is above zero and Discreet mode is off (the status pill does not say QUIET);
   2. SETUP > Find alert is not Off;
   3. SETUP > Self-test > Sound is green; tap BEEP.
   In Track, QUIET silences the beeps and SOUND brings them back.

E. Wi-Fi or Bluetooth finds are missing or slow
   - Make sure a Wi-Fi group (not ZIGBEE/THREAD) is armed. A tile that is dim red and says "blocked" means you armed
     groups from the other side of the radio rule: switch the other side off first.
   - Wi-Fi pauses while the Share hotspot is on.
   - The list says "Wi-Fi radio: no frames yet" at first, then after 10 seconds "scanning access points". In that
     mode only access points are seen, in bursts.
   - Bluetooth finds slow while a Wi-Fi group is armed: the watch has one radio for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Turn on
     SETUP > Bluetooth share so Wi-Fi pauses regularly and Bluetooth gets the whole radio. Arming only Bluetooth
     groups also gives Bluetooth the whole radio.
   - The watch listens on 2.4 GHz only. A device on 5 GHz Wi-Fi is not heard.
   - If SETUP > Self-test > Radios shows "restarted in:" and a step name, the watch restarted unexpectedly. Please
     report it with that line.

F. Log times are blank or amber, or the watch face says "clock not set"
   The clock is not set. Open the Live view from your phone once (help/1_GETTING_STARTED.txt).

G. The phone will not join the watch's Wi-Fi / will not stay connected
   - Join the exact name shown on the watch, with the password shown.
   - If the phone refuses, forget any older saved network called HUNTR-SEEKR (iPhone: Settings > Wi-Fi > the i next to
     the name > Forget This Network; Android: touch and hold the name > Forget) and join again.
   - Some phones drop networks without internet: turn off "auto-switch to mobile data" while connected.
   - The address is http://192.168.4.1 (not https).

H. AIM says GYRO NOT WORKING / NO CLEAR SIDE, or the arrow turns the wrong way
   See section 6.12 and the troubleshooting table in the user guide (docs folder). In short: open SETUP > Self-test >
   Motion (it should show changing numbers), move to open space, hold the watch the same way throughout, and tap REDO
   (or FLIP if the arrow turns the wrong way).

I. A watch or phone shows only "Apple", or does not appear
   Normal. Apple devices rotate their address and send very little, so they cannot be told apart.

J. Reset everything
   Flash the .bin again (help/2_FLASHING.txt): this resets the saved settings. Saved logs are kept.

Still stuck? Open an issue (Bug report form) with the release name from the boot screen and the Serial Monitor output.
