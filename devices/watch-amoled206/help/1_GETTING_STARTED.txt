1. GETTING STARTED
==================

What the board is
-----------------
The Waveshare ESP32-C6-Touch-AMOLED-2.06 is a small board with an ESP32-C6 chip, a 2.06 inch AMOLED touch screen,
a speaker, a motion sensor, a clock chip and a battery connector. HUNTR/SEEKR is the software that runs on it.

Parts you may want to add (all optional)
----------------------------------------
  Battery      A lithium battery of the type Waveshare specifies for the board. Without one it runs from USB power.
  GPS          A module connected to the board's UART pads; it adds your position to the log.
  Phone        Used for the Live view, to set the clock and to update the software, over the watch's own Wi-Fi.

Step by step
------------
 1. Put the software on the watch: help/2_FLASHING.txt.
 2. The boot screen ends with "ready  Public Beta 1". A few seconds later the ARM page appears with nine group tiles
    (ALPR, AXON, CAMS, GLASS, DRONE, BT, TAGS, WIFI, ZIGBEE/THREAD).
 3. Tap a tile to arm that group. Swipe left and right, or tap the icons along the bottom, to move between the
    pages: ARM, FINDS, SWEEP, GROUPS, LOG and SETUP.
 4. Open FINDS to see what has been heard. Tap a device, then TRACK to follow its signal: the number closer to
    zero means closer.
 5. Open SETUP > Self-test. Every dot should be green. Grey means a part your watch does not have. Clock and
    Battery can be amber until a clock is set or a battery is fitted.

The clock
---------
Press SHARE on the LOG page, join the watch's Wi-Fi hotspot with your phone (the name and password are shown on the
screen) and open http://192.168.4.1 (not https). The watch takes the time and time zone from the phone, so your
saved logs have time stamps.

Next

The user guide (PDF in the docs folder) explains every screen. If something does not work: help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt.
