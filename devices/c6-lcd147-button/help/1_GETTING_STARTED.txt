1. GETTING STARTED
==================

What the board is
-----------------
The Waveshare ESP32-C6-LCD-1.47 is a small board with an ESP32-C6 chip, a 1.47 inch screen (172 x 320), an RGB LED,
a BOOT button and a RESET button. It has NO touch panel, no motion sensor, no speaker and no clock chip.
HUNTR/SEEKR is the software that runs on it. (The board that has a touch screen has "Touch" in its name; it uses the
touch edition of the software.)

The one button
--------------
Everything is done with the BOOT button. How long you hold it decides what it does, and it acts when you let go:
  TAP   (under half a second)     NEXT     move to the next item
  HOLD  (half a second to 1.5 s)  SELECT   open / start / apply the highlighted item
  LONG  (over 1.5 seconds)        BACK     go back (on the home screen: screen off, any press wakes it)
The three boxes along the bottom of every screen show what each gesture does now, and the box for the gesture you are
making lights up while the button is down. The RGB LED changes colour at the same moments (teal-green past half a
second, violet past one and a half), so it also works by feel.

Parts you may want to add (all optional)
----------------------------------------
  Buzzer       A small PASSIVE buzzer or piezo between a free header pin and GND; put that pin in PIN_BUZZER in the
               sketch (user guide, section 10). Without it the board makes no sound.
  Buttons      Up to two extra push buttons between free header pins and GND (PIN_BTN_EXT1, PIN_BTN_EXT2) make
               the interface quicker: EXT1 = NEXT, EXT2 = SELECT, BOOT = BACK.
  GPS          A module connected to the board's GPS pads; it adds your position to the log.
  Phone        Used for the Live view, to set the clock (the board has no clock chip) and to download saved logs.

Step by step
------------
 1. Put the software on the board: help/2_FLASHING.txt.
 2. The boot screen ends with "ready  Public Beta 1". A few seconds later the Home screen appears. It says IDLE.
 3. TAP twice to move the highlight to SCENE, then HOLD to open the scene list.
 4. TAP to the scene you want (for example STREET) and HOLD to arm it. The Home screen says LISTENING and the LED
    gives a slow teal blip every few seconds.
 5. When something is found a banner drops down and the LED pulses in the group's colour. TAP skips the banner,
    HOLD tracks that device.
 6. To see everything heard: on Home, highlight FINDS and HOLD. TAP walks down the list, HOLD tracks the highlighted
    device (a number closer to zero means closer), LONG goes back.
 7. To put the screen away: LONG on the Home screen. Scanning goes on; any press wakes the screen.

The clock
---------
The board has no clock chip. Choose More > Phone page (TAP to MORE, HOLD, HOLD on Phone page), join the board's Wi-Fi
hotspot with your phone (the name and password are shown on the screen) and open http://192.168.4.1 (not https), then
open the Live view. The board takes the time and time zone from the phone, so your saved logs have time stamps.

Saved findings
--------------
Findings are saved automatically, every five minutes and when scanning stops. Download them from the same phone page
(the file list on its first screen).

Hardware status
---------------
This release has been flashed and run on a real plain ESP32-C6-LCD-1.47 by the project: it starts, shows the interface and finds Wi-Fi networks. An earlier one-button build of the same interface (version 3.6-btn) also ran on that board, including the display settings, the LED and reading BOOT as the button. Not every feature has been tried one by one on this release yet (Bluetooth, Zigbee/Thread, the phone page and the saved logs in particular). Please report what you see, good or bad, with the Bug report form.
