3. TROUBLESHOOTING
==================

The board prints start-up messages over USB that help a lot: open any serial terminal at 115200 baud on its port.

A. The computer does not see the board / flashing fails
   - Use a USB DATA cable. Try another USB port on the computer.
   - Hold the board's BOOT button while plugging the cable in, release it, then try again.
   - If the connection is unreliable, add --baud 115200 to the command.

B. Nothing shows on the screen
   - Press the button once: the screen may have gone to sleep (LONG on the Home screen turns it off, any press
     wakes it). The first press after the screen is off only wakes it.
   - Unplug the cable, wait 10 seconds, plug it in again. Try another cable or USB port.
   - Flash the .bin again (help/2_FLASHING.txt).
   - The Serial Monitor (115200) prints "free heap ... fs ok" at start-up. If it does not, the program did not start.

C. The picture is wrong
   - It looks like a photo negative (colours inverted): in the sketch set LCD_IPS to 0, then upload again.
   - It is shifted sideways or has a coloured edge: change LCD_COL_OFS (34 is the usual value for this 172 pixel
     wide panel; try 0 or 35), then upload again.
   (Both are #defines near the top of the sketch. You need to build the sketch yourself to change them: docs/BUILDING.md.)

D. The button does nothing, or does the wrong thing
   - BOOT is the only button the software reads. RESET only restarts the chip.
   - A button that is already held when the board powers up does nothing until it has been let go once.
   - When the screen is off or dimmed the first press only wakes it.
   - A press longer than 9 seconds is treated as a stuck button and ignored until it is released.
   - TAP is under half a second, HOLD is half a second to 1.5 seconds, LONG is over 1.5 seconds. Watch the three
     boxes at the bottom: the one for your gesture lights up while the button is down. Let go while the box you want is lit.
   - Too quick or too slow for you? BTN_SEL_MS (500) and BTN_BACK_MS (1500) in the sketch.

E. The LED never lights
   - Check More > Alerts: LED OFF and DISCREET switch it off. The LED is also dark while nothing is armed.
   - If the sketch will not build at the LED lines, update the esp32 board package to 3.3.5 or newer
     (the sketch uses rgbLedWrite()).

F. No sound
   The board has no speaker. You need a PASSIVE buzzer between a free header pin and GND, with that pin number in
   PIN_BUZZER in the sketch (it is -1, meaning none, by default). With it fitted, More > Alerts has sound choices.

G. Wi-Fi or Bluetooth finds are missing or slow
   - Make sure a Wi-Fi group (not ZB/TH) is armed. A group that says "blocked" means you armed groups from the other
     side of the radio rule: switch the other side off first (ZB/TH and the Wi-Fi groups cannot run together).
   - Wi-Fi pauses while the phone page (Share) is on.
   - The list says "Wi-Fi radio: no frames yet" at first. After a few seconds it falls back to scanning access points,
     which only sees them in bursts.
   - Bluetooth finds slow while a Wi-Fi group is armed: the board has one radio for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Arming only
     Bluetooth groups (BT, TAGS) gives Bluetooth the whole radio.
   - The board listens on 2.4 GHz only. A device on 5 GHz Wi-Fi is not heard.

H. Log times are blank
   The clock is not set. Open the Live view from your phone once (help/1_GETTING_STARTED.txt).

I. The phone will not join the board's Wi-Fi / will not stay connected
   - Join the exact name shown on the board (it ends in four characters that change every time), with the password shown.
   - If the phone refuses, forget any older saved network called HUNTR-SEEKR (iPhone: Settings > Wi-Fi > the i next to
     the name > Forget This Network; Android: touch and hold the name > Forget) and join again.
   - Some phones drop networks without internet: turn off "auto-switch to mobile data" while connected.
   - The address is http://192.168.4.1 (not https).

J. The phone update page does not work
   Not supported on this board in this release. Update over USB (help/2_FLASHING.txt).

K. A watch or phone shows only "Apple", or does not appear
   Normal. Apple devices rotate their address and send very little, so they cannot be told apart.

L. Reset everything
   Flash the .bin again (help/2_FLASHING.txt): this resets the saved settings. Saved logs are kept; wipe them from the
   phone page if you want.

Still stuck? Open an issue (Bug report form) with the release name from the boot screen and the Serial Monitor output.
