4. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
=============================

Does it transmit anything?
  No attacks, no jamming, no joining other people's networks. It listens. It does run a small Wi-Fi hotspot of its own
  (with a password) while the phone page is on, so you can use it from a phone.

Does it find everything?
  No. It matches what it hears against built-in lists. Names and addresses can be changed or randomized, many devices
  are not in the lists, and a quiet device may be out of range. "Nothing found" never proves nothing is there, and a
  match is a hint, not proof.

Which board is this for?
  The plain Waveshare ESP32-C6-LCD-1.47, the one WITHOUT a touch panel. The board with a touch screen has its own
  edition (folder c6-lcd147).

Why one button?
  BOOT is the only button on the board that software can read. TAP moves to the next item, HOLD selects, LONG goes back,
  and the strip along the bottom of every screen shows what each one does.

Can it tell me which direction a signal comes from?
  Not in this edition. The board has one antenna and no motion sensor, so there is no direction finder (AIM). Track
  shows signal strength (dBm): the number closer to zero means closer, and the LED blinks faster as you get closer.

Does it scan 5 GHz Wi-Fi?
  No. This chip has 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only.

Why do Bluetooth finds sometimes appear slowly?
  Wi-Fi and Bluetooth share one radio. With both armed, each hears a little less than it would alone. Arming only
  Bluetooth groups gives Bluetooth the whole radio.

Why is there no sound?
  The board has no speaker. Add a small passive buzzer between a free pin and GND and set PIN_BUZZER (user guide, section 10).

Does it need a battery?
  No: it runs from USB power. The board has no battery gauge, so the software shows no battery level.

Does it save what it finds?
  Yes, automatically: the findings record is written to the board's flash every five minutes and when scanning stops.
  Download the files from the phone page (More > Phone page).

Can I add my own devices to detect?
  Yes: your own rules ("signatures") can be added from your phone, up to 128; see the user guide, section 9.3. A signature
  update file (sigs_update.txt) uploaded from the same page is read after them.

How do I update?
  Flash the newer .bin over USB (help/2_FLASHING.txt). The phone update page is not supported on this board in this release.

Is it legal to use?
  Receiving openly broadcast radio signals is lawful in many places, but laws differ. You are responsible for following
  the laws where you are. Do not use it to identify, follow or harass people.

Where do I report a problem or suggest a device?
  On the project page: Issues tab, Bug report or Feature request form.
