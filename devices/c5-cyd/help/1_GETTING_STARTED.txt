1. GETTING STARTED
==================

What the board is
-----------------
The NM-CYD-C5 is a small board with an ESP32-C5 chip, a 2.8 inch resistive touch screen (240 x 320), a microSD
card slot and USB-C ports. HUNTR/SEEKR is the software that runs on it.

Parts you may want to add (all optional)
----------------------------------------
  Buzzer       A small PASSIVE buzzer or piezo. Header P1: pin 3 (IO26) to its +, pin 4 (GND) to its -.
               Without it the board makes no sound. A passive piezo gives the best result.
               IO26 is one of the chip's boot-mode pins: if flashing ever fails with a magnetic buzzer fitted,
               unplug it while you flash. Do not use IO8 or IO9 (the I2C lines).
  microSD      FAT32, 32 GB or smaller is safest. Fit it BEFORE you switch the board on (it is not noticed later).
               Logs, the ignore list and your own rules are kept on the card; with no card they go to the board's flash.
  GPS          A module such as the NM-ATGM336H plugs into the board's P5 socket and adds your position to the log.
  Phone        Used for the Live view and to set the clock (the board has no clock chip) over the board's own Wi-Fi.

Step by step
------------
 1. Put the software on the board: help/2_FLASHING.txt.
 2. After the boot screen (it ends with "ready  Public Beta 1") the first start asks you to calibrate touch.
    Press and hold the cross near each corner until the bar fills and the cross turns green, then lift. Four crosses.
    A stylus or fingernail tip works best. If it says TRY AGAIN, just repeat it. When it says "Touch calibrated",
    tap CONTINUE.
 3. You land on the ARM page: nine group tiles (ALPR, AXON, CAMS, GLASS, DRONE, BT, TAGS, WIFI, ZIGBEE/THREAD).
    Tap a tile to arm that group. Use the tabs at the bottom to move between ARM, FINDS, GROUPS, LOG and SETUP.
 4. Open FINDS to see what has been heard. Tap a device, then TRACK to follow its signal: the number closer to
    zero means closer.
 5. Open SETUP > Self-test. Every dot should be green except Motion (this board has no motion sensor) and Clock
    (until you set it from your phone).

The clock
---------
The board has no clock chip. Press SHARE on the LOG page, join the board's Wi-Fi hotspot with your phone (the name
and password are shown on the screen) and open http://192.168.4.1 (not https). The board takes the time and time zone
from the phone, so your saved logs have time stamps.
