HELP - FIND YOUR PROBLEM FAST
=============================

I want to put the software on my board ..................... help/2_FLASHING.txt
The computer does not see the board ........................ Use a USB-C DATA cable. Try the other USB-C port and another
                                                              USB port. Hold BOOT while plugging in. (help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, A)
The screen is black ......................................... help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, B
Colours are wrong ............................................ help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, B
Touch does nothing / lands in the wrong place ............... SETUP > Calibrate touch (help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, C)
No sound ..................................................... Needs a passive buzzer on IO26 and GND (help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, D)
Wi-Fi finds nothing ......................................... help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, E
Logs are not saved to the SD card ........................... help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, F
My phone cannot join the board's Wi-Fi ...................... help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, G
How do I update to a newer release? ......................... Flash the new .bin again (help/2_FLASHING.txt)
Anything else ................................................ help/4_FAQ.txt and the user guide (docs folder)

If it is still stuck, open an issue on the project page (Bug report form). Include the release name shown on the
boot screen, what you did, and what the Serial Monitor (115200 baud) printed.
