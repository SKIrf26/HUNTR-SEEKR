1. GETTING STARTED
==================

What the board is
-----------------
The Waveshare ESP32-C6-Touch-LCD-1.47 is a small board with an ESP32-C6 chip, a 1.47 inch touch screen (172 x 320), a
motion sensor and a battery connector. It has no speaker and no clock chip. HUNTR/SEEKR is the software that runs on it.

Parts you may want to add (all optional)
----------------------------------------
  Buzzer       A small PASSIVE buzzer or piezo: its + leg to pad IO7, its - leg to GND. Without it the board makes no sound.
  Battery      A lithium battery of the type Waveshare specifies for the board. Without one it runs from USB power.
  GPS          A module connected to the board's GPS pads; it adds your position to the log.
  Phone        Used for the Live view and to set the clock (the board has no clock chip) over the board's own Wi-Fi.

Step by step
------------
 1. Put the software on the board: help/2_FLASHING.txt.
 2. The boot screen ends with "ready  Public Beta 1". A few seconds later the ARM page appears with nine group tiles (ALPR, AXON, CAMS, GLASS, DRONE, BT, TAGS, WIFI, ZIGBEE/THREAD).
 3. Tap a tile to arm that group. Swipe left and right, or tap the icons along the bottom, to move between the
    pages: ARM, FINDS, SWEEP, GROUPS, LOG and SETUP.
 4. Open FINDS to see what has been heard. Tap a device, then TRACK to follow its signal: the number closer to
    zero means closer.
 5. Open SETUP > Self-test. Every dot should be green. Grey means a part your board does not have. Clock is
    amber until you set it from your phone, and Battery until one is fitted.

The clock
---------
The board has no clock chip. Press SHARE on the LOG page, join the board's Wi-Fi hotspot with your phone (the name
and password are shown on the screen) and open http://192.168.4.1 (not https). The board takes the time and time zone
from the phone, so your saved logs have time stamps.

Hardware status
---------------
This release has been run on the real board by the project (start-up, scanning and the Track screen); the
other screens were checked in a desktop simulation of the interface, so some features have had less testing on
hardware. Please report what you
see, good or bad, with the Bug report form.
