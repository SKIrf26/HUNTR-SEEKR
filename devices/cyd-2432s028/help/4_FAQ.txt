4. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
=============================

Does it transmit anything?
  No attacks, no jamming, no joining other people's networks. It listens. It does run a small Wi-Fi hotspot of its own
  (with a password) when you press SHARE, so you can use it from a phone.

Does it find everything?
  No. It matches what it hears against built-in lists. Names and addresses can be changed or randomized, many devices
  are not in the lists, and a quiet device may be out of range. "Nothing found" never proves nothing is there, and a
  match is a hint, not proof.

Can it tell me which direction a signal comes from?
  No. The board has one antenna. Track shows signal strength (dBm): the number closer to zero means closer. Walk around
  and watch whether it gets warmer or colder.

Does it scan 5 GHz Wi-Fi?
  No. The ESP32 has 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only.

Why do Bluetooth finds sometimes appear slowly?
  Wi-Fi and Bluetooth share one radio. With both armed, each hears a little less than it would alone. Turn on
  SETUP > Bluetooth share to give Bluetooth the whole radio for a while in every few seconds.

Why is there no sound?
  Most boards are sold without a speaker. Plug a small 8 ohm speaker into the speaker socket.

Does it need a battery?
  No: it runs from USB power. The board has no battery or clock chip.

Does it save what it finds?
  When you choose SAVE LOG on the LOG page, the findings record is written to the microSD card (or the board's flash).
  Use a FAT32 card, fitted before you switch the board on.

Can I add my own devices to detect?
  Yes: your own rules ("signatures") can be added, up to 128; see the user guide, section 9.3. A sigs_update.txt file
  on the microSD card is read as well, so a list from someone else can simply be copied onto the card.

How do I update?
  Flash the newer .bin over USB (help/2_FLASHING.txt). The phone update page is not supported on this board in
  this release.

Is it legal to use?
  Receiving openly broadcast radio signals is lawful in many places, but laws differ. You are responsible for following
  the laws where you are. Do not use it to identify, follow or harass people.

Where do I report a problem or suggest a device?
  On the project page: Issues tab, Bug report or Feature request form.
