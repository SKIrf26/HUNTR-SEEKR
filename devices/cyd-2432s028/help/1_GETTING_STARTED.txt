1. GETTING STARTED
==================

What the board is
-----------------
The ESP32-2432S028 (the "CYD 2.8", sold as the "cheap yellow display") is a small board with a classic ESP32 chip, a 2.8 inch
resistive touch screen (240 x 320), a microSD card slot, a speaker socket and a USB port. HUNTR/SEEKR is the software that runs on it.

Parts you may want to add (all optional)
----------------------------------------
  Speaker      A small 8 ohm speaker (1 watt is plenty) in the board's two-pin speaker socket. Without one the board makes
               no sound. The software drives the amplifier from IO26.
  microSD      FAT32, 32 GB or smaller is safest. Fit it BEFORE you switch the board on (it is not noticed later).
               Logs, the ignore list and your own rules are kept on the card; with no card they go to the board's flash.
  GPS          A module on the 4-pin CN1 connector: its TX to IO27, its RX to IO22, plus 3V3 and GND (9600 baud).
  Phone        Used for the Live view and to set the clock (the board has no clock chip) over the board's own Wi-Fi.

Step by step
------------
 1. Put the software on the board: help/2_FLASHING.txt.
 2. After the boot screen (it ends with "ready  Public Beta 1") the first start asks you to calibrate touch.
    Press and hold the cross near each corner until the bar fills and the cross turns green, then lift. Four crosses.
    A stylus or fingernail tip works best. If it says TRY AGAIN, just repeat it. When it says "Touch calibrated",
    tap CONTINUE.
 3. You land on the ARM page: eight group tiles (ALPR, AXON, CAMS, GLASS, DRONE, BT, TAGS, WIFI).
    Tap a tile to arm that group. Swipe left and right, or tap the tabs along the bottom, to move between ARM, FINDS, GROUPS, LOG and SETUP.
 4. Open FINDS to see what has been heard. Tap a device, then TRACK to follow its signal: the number closer to
    zero means closer.
 5. Open SETUP > Self-test. Every dot should be green. Motion is grey and says "not fitted" (this board has no motion
    sensor), and Clock is amber until you set it from your phone.

The clock
---------
The board has no clock chip. Press SHARE on the LOG page, join the board's Wi-Fi hotspot with your phone (the name
and password are shown on the screen) and open http://192.168.4.1 (not https). The board takes the time and time zone
from the phone, so your saved logs have time stamps.

Hardware status
---------------
This release builds cleanly for the ESP32-2432S028 (CYD 2.8), and its screens were checked in a desktop
simulation of the interface. It has not yet been run on the real board by the project. Please report what you
see, good or bad, with the Bug report form.
