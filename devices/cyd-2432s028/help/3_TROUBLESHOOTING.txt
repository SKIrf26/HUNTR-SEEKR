3. TROUBLESHOOTING
==================

The board prints start-up messages over USB that help a lot: open any serial terminal at 115200 baud on the board's port.
The board's own check: SETUP > Self-test (green dot = healthy, red dot = not). On this board Motion is grey (no motion
sensor) and Clock is amber until you set it from your phone. Both are normal.

A. The computer does not see the board / flashing fails
   - Use a USB DATA cable. Try another USB port on the computer.
   - If no port appears, install the free driver for the board's USB chip (WCH CH340, or Silicon Labs CP210x).
   - Hold the board's BOOT button while plugging the cable in, release it, then try again.
   - If the connection is unreliable, add --baud 115200 to the command.

B. Nothing shows on the screen, or the colours are wrong
   - Unplug the board, wait 10 seconds, plug it in again. Try another cable or USB port.
   - Flash the .bin again (help/2_FLASHING.txt).
   - If it stays black or shows noise or wrong colours, your board may have a different display panel than this release
     expects. Look in config/ for the alternative display set-up files (needed only if you build the firmware
     yourself), and please report it (Bug report form) with a photo of the board's back and the Serial Monitor output.

C. Touch does nothing, or lands in the wrong place
   - The boot screen says "touch NOT detected": unplug, wait, plug in again. Check SETUP > Self-test > Touch.
   - Taps land beside the target, or only work near the edges: SETUP > Calibrate touch, and press each cross exactly
     (a stylus or fingernail tip helps).
   - The first touch after a pause does nothing: normal, that touch wakes the screen.

D. No sound
   The board has a small amplifier and a speaker socket, but most units are sold without a speaker. Check in this order:
   1. a small 8 ohm speaker is plugged into the speaker socket;
   2. the Volume slider is up and Discreet mode is off (a long-press on the status pill toggles it);
   3. SETUP > Self-test > BEEP plays a tone.
   In Track, QUIET silences the beeps and SOUND brings them back.

E. Wi-Fi or Bluetooth finds are missing or slow
   - Make sure the group you expect is armed on the ARM page.
   - Wi-Fi pauses while the Share hotspot is on.
   - Bluetooth finds slow while a Wi-Fi group is armed: the board has one radio for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Turn on
     SETUP > Bluetooth share so Wi-Fi pauses regularly and Bluetooth gets the whole radio. Arming only Bluetooth
     groups also gives Bluetooth the whole radio.
   - The board listens on 2.4 GHz only. A device on 5 GHz Wi-Fi is not heard.

F. Logs are not saved to the microSD card
   - SETUP > Self-test, drag down to the SD card row: it says whether the card was found.
   - Fit the card BEFORE switching the board on. Use FAT32 (cards over 32 GB are often exFAT, which the board cannot
     read). SETUP > Save to must not be "Flash only".

G. The phone will not join the board's Wi-Fi / will not stay connected
   - Join the exact name shown on the board: it ends in four characters that change every time you press SHARE,
     together with the password shown.
   - If the phone refuses, forget any older saved network called HUNTR-SEEKR (iPhone: Settings > Wi-Fi > the i next to
     the name > Forget This Network; Android: touch and hold the name > Forget) and join again.
   - Some phones drop networks without internet: turn off "auto-switch to mobile data" while connected.
   - The address is http://192.168.4.1 (not https).

H. The phone update page does not work
   Not supported on this board in this release. Update over USB (help/2_FLASHING.txt).

I. A watch or phone shows only "Apple", or does not appear
   Normal. Apple devices rotate their address and send very little, so they cannot be told apart.

J. Reset everything
   Flash the .bin again (help/2_FLASHING.txt): this resets the saved settings. Logs on the microSD card are not touched.

Still stuck? Open an issue (Bug report form) with the release name from the boot screen and the Serial Monitor output.
