3. TROUBLESHOOTING
==================

The board prints start-up messages over USB that help a lot: open any serial terminal at 115200 baud on the board's port.
The board's own check: SETUP > Self-test (green dot = healthy, red dot = not). On this board Motion says "no sensor on this board" (normal) and Clock is amber until it is set (also normal).

A. The computer does not see the board / flashing fails
   - Use a USB DATA cable. Try another USB port on the computer.
   - If no port appears, install the free driver for the board's USB chip (the page of the board names it).
   - Hold the BOOT button while plugging the cable in, release it, then try again.
   - If the connection is unreliable, add --baud 115200 to the command.

B. Nothing shows on the screen, or the colours are wrong
   - Unplug the board, wait 10 seconds, plug it in again. Try another cable or USB port.
   - Flash the .bin again (help/2_FLASHING.txt).
   - The backlight comes on only after the first picture is ready. If the Serial Monitor shows "TCA9554 ... NOT FOUND" the
     display and touch resets could not be released: check that you flashed the right image for this board.
   - Colours like a photo negative: this needs a one-word change in the sketch (config/README.md) and a rebuild. Please
     report it with the Serial Monitor output.

C. Touch does nothing, or lands in the wrong place
   - The Serial Monitor line "FT6336 ... found" confirms the touch chip answers. SETUP > Self-test > Touch shows the same.
   - Taps land mirrored or swapped: set TP_SWAP_XY / TP_FLIP_X / TP_FLIP_Y near the top of the sketch (config/README.md) and
     rebuild. Please tell us which combination was right, so the next release can have it as the default.
   - The first touch after a pause does nothing: normal, that touch wakes the screen.

D. No sound
   1. the Volume slider is up and Discreet mode is off (a long-press on the status pill toggles it);
   2. SETUP > Self-test > BEEP plays a tone;
   3. the Self-test Sound row says whether the audio codec was found.
   In Track, QUIET silences the beeps and SOUND brings them back.

E. Wi-Fi or Bluetooth finds are missing or slow
   - Make sure the group you expect is armed on the ARM page.
   - Wi-Fi pauses while the Share hotspot is on.
   - Bluetooth finds slow while a Wi-Fi group is armed: the board has one radio for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Turn on
     SETUP > Bluetooth share so Wi-Fi pauses regularly and Bluetooth gets the whole radio. Arming only Bluetooth
     groups also gives Bluetooth the whole radio.
   - The board listens on 2.4 GHz only. A device on 5 GHz Wi-Fi is not heard.
   - "Bluetooth off: low memory" on the screen: the extra memory (PSRAM) is not switched on in this build. Use the ready-made
     .bin, or choose Tools > PSRAM > Enabled when building.

F. Logs are not saved to the TF card
   - Fit the card BEFORE switching the board on. Use FAT32 (cards over 32 GB are often exFAT, which the board cannot read).
   - The Serial Monitor says "SD card ok" at start-up when the card is found; otherwise it says it is using internal flash.

G. The phone will not join the board's Wi-Fi / will not stay connected
   - Join the exact name shown on the board, with the password shown.
   - If the phone refuses, forget any older saved network called HUNTR-SEEKR (iPhone: Settings > Wi-Fi > the i next to
     the name > Forget This Network; Android: touch and hold the name > Forget) and join again.
   - Some phones drop networks without internet: turn off "auto-switch to mobile data" while connected.
   - The address is http://192.168.4.1 (not https).

H. The phone update page does not work
   It needs the 16 MB image (two app slots). The 4 MB image is updated over USB (help/2_FLASHING.txt).

I. A phone or watch shows only "Apple", or does not appear
   Normal. Apple devices rotate their address and send very little, so they cannot be told apart.

J. Reset everything
   Flash the .bin again (help/2_FLASHING.txt): this resets the saved settings. Logs on the TF card are not touched.

Still stuck? Open an issue (Bug report form) with the release name from the boot screen and the Serial Monitor output.
