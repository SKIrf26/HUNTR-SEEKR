4. FAQ
======

Is this tested on the real board?
  This first release for the board ("Port Test 6") is a port of the current firmware that has not yet been run on the
  hardware. It compiles and follows Waveshare's own pin map and examples. Please report what you find.

Does it transmit anything?
  No. It only listens. The board's own Wi-Fi hotspot is used only when you press SHARE, to show the live view on your phone.

Why eight groups and not nine?
  The chip on this board has no 802.15.4 radio, so Zigbee and Thread cannot be heard. That group is left out.

Why is there no AIM (direction arrow)?
  AIM needs a motion sensor (gyro). This board has none. Walk toward the stronger signal using the Track screen.

Can it see 5 GHz Wi-Fi?
  No. The chip is 2.4 GHz only.

Where are the logs?
  On the TF card when one is fitted at start-up, otherwise in the board's flash. Open the LOG page, or use SHARE and the phone page.

Why does it need the extra memory (PSRAM)?
  The classic ESP32 has limited fast memory, and Wi-Fi and Bluetooth use most of it. The firmware keeps its large lists in the
  board's 2 MB of PSRAM. The ready-made .bin is built for that.

Is the battery needed?
  No. USB power is enough. With a battery fitted the status bar shows its charge.

How do I turn it off?
  Hold the PWR button for the Power screen (sleep or restart), or hold BOOT for the sleep countdown. Touch or a button wakes it.

Can I change the screen look or the groups?
  Yes: see SETUP. Custom rules (SSID, name, address prefix, company id, service id) can be added from the phone page.

More questions: open an issue on the project page.
